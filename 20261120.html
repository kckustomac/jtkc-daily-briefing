Word of the Day

Merriam-Webster · November 20, 2026
Wordruthless
Pronunciation\ROOTH-lus\

adjective — having no pity; describes people, behaviors, or circumstances regarded as cruel or merciless.

“Wolves and other large carnivores are often portrayed (and feared) as ruthless predators even as they play vital roles in their ecosystems.”

In context: “One of Shakespeare's most powerful and enduring tragedies, 'Macbeth' remains an urgent and gripping story. Ambition and prophecy collide as a fateful prediction drives Macbeth into a ruthless pursuit of power, destroying everything in his path.” — The Daily Gazette (Schenectady, New York), 25 Sept. 2026

Did you know? Ruthless means “without ruth,” and ruth is an old word for compassion or remorse. Its antonym is ruthful, “full of ruth” or tender. Both trace back to Middle English ruthe, from ruwen, “to rue.”
