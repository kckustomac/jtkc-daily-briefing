On This Day

November 27, 1895

Alfred Nobel signs his will, creating the Nobel Prize. On this day in 1895 the Swedish chemist and dynamite inventor signed his last will and set aside the bulk of his fortune to fund the prizes that now carry his name. Relatives contested it, and the first prizes were not awarded until 1901.

Word of the Day

Merriam-Webster · November 27, 2026

injunction · \in-JUNK-shun\ · noun

Meaning: An order from a court of law that says something must be done or must not be done.

We requested an injunction with hopes of preserving the historic landmark.

“… an Indiana Circuit Court judge in September granted him and a group of former college athletes a preliminary injunction that would allow an additional year of eligibility.” — Matt Murshel, The Orlando Sentinel, 19 Oct. 2026

Did you know? Injunction comes from Latin injungere (“to enjoin”), built on jungere, “to join,” which also gave us junction, conjunction and join. Merriam-Webster

Congress: Congress is on Thanksgiving break, with lawmakers due back next week. The big deadline is Dec. 11, when federal funding expires: they must pass spending bills or a short-term extension to avoid a shutdown. The White House has floated a stopgap running through March 31, and a fight is brewing over whether to restrict further Iran-war funding in exchange for more defense money.
