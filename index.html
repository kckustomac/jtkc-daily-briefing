On This Day

November 14, 1969

Apollo 12 launches and gets hit by lightning. Astronauts Charles Conrad Jr., Richard Gordon Jr. and Alan Bean lifted off from Cape Canaveral on NASA's second Moon mission, with President Nixon watching from the pad, the first president to attend a crewed launch. Word of the Day

Merriam-Webster · November 14, 2026

boilerplate

noun · \BOY-ler-playt\

Audio: Merriam-Webster's official Word of the Day podcast episode (pronunciation and usage).

What it means: Boilerplate refers to standard or formulaic language, a meaning that comes from an earlier meaning still in use: syndicated material supplied to newspapers in matrix or plate form.

// The last paragraph of the contract was legal boilerplate.

In context: “New Orleans is where the food, music, history and culture are unlike anywhere else in the world. That’s not travel-guide boilerplate—it’s what hits you the moment you step into the French Quarter and feel the city operating on its own rhythm.” — Lauren Schuster, The Miami Herald, 8 Apr. 2026

Did you know? Before computers, small newspapers leaned on features, editorials and other material from big syndicates, which shipped it on metal plates with the type already set. Printers dubbed those plates “boiler plates” for their resemblance to the plating used in steam boilers. Because boilerplate stories were often filler rather than news, the word picked up negative connotations and the “standardized or formulaic language” sense used today.

More than 500 GM Fairfax workers to be temporarily laid off

A WARN notice filed Nov. 10 says 506 workers at General Motors' Fairfax Assembly and Stamping Plant in Kansas City, Kansas, will be temporarily laid off. The notice doesn't say which jobs or when it starts; UAW Local 31 represents the workers, and the plant builds the Chevrolet Bolt, Equinox and a Buick compact crossover.

FAA lifts nationwide ground stop that affected Kansas City airports

The FAA halted departing flights nationwide Friday over a telecommunications outage affecting air traffic control centers, holding outbound flights at KCI and Wheeler Downtown before lifting the order; the agency said every flight was accounted for. The stop disrupted travelers and teams, including the Kansas City Current's flight to New York for Sunday's match.

KCTV5 · Nov 13 · Read story

Erin's House 2 opens in KCK to serve domestic violence survivors

A new six-bedroom transitional home opened Friday in Kansas City, Kansas, giving five women a place to stay for 90 days to a year. It's named for counselor Erin Langhofer, killed by a stray bullet at a 2019 First Fridays event, and was built by the Do Good for Erin foundation with Friends of Yates, which raised more than $400,000 for it.
