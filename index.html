On This Day

November 17 · 1970

The computer mouse gets its patent. On this day in 1970, Douglas Engelbart was granted U.S. Patent 3,541,541 for the “X-Y Position Indicator for a Display System” — a hand-held block with two perpendicular wheels that steered a cursor on screen. The application had been filed June 21, 1967. Every click you make descends from it.

Word of the Day

Merriam-Webster · November 17, 2026
Wordstolid
PronunciationSTAH-lid

adjective — having or expressing little or no emotion.

“The detective presented the facts of the case with stolid indifference.”

In the news: “Even the most stolid of personalities can get dragged into drama, breaking one of the biggest rules in journalism: never become the story.” — Katie Rosseinsky, The Independent, 15 Apr. 2026

Did you know? Stolid comes from Latin stolidus, “dull” or “stupid,” and is distantly related to stultify. It once carried that insult in the 1600s; by the 1800s it just meant unflappable.

Kansas City had its 4th-warmest fall ever — making electricity bills higher than usual

Fall ran 7–8 degrees above normal, the fourth-warmest in 139 years of KC records, and the first freeze only arrived this past weekend.
