Word of the Day

Merriam-Webster · November 10, 2026

defer

verb · \dih-FER\

Audio: Merriam-Webster's official Word of the Day podcast episode (pronunciation and usage).

What it means: To defer something is to put it off or delay it, to choose to do it at a later time.

// We decided to defer the offer so that we could explore other options.

In context: “Amelie plans to defer college for a year to attempt a mostly solo thru-hike of the 2,650-mile Pacific Crest Trail.” — Justin Souza, Noozhawk (Santa Barbara, California), 24 Sept. 2026

Did you know? There are two distinct words spelled defer in English. The one meaning to put off or delay comes from the Latin differre (“to postpone, to delay”), which also gave us differ. The one meaning to yield to someone else’s decision (“deferred to his parents’ wishes”) comes from a different Latin verb, dēferre, “to bring down, convey, transfer, submit.”
