Word of the Day

Merriam-Webster · November 25, 2026

arbiter · \AHR-buh-ter\ · noun

Meaning: A person with power to decide a dispute, or a person or agency whose judgment or opinion is considered authoritative.

The mayor will act as the final arbiter in any disputes between board members.

He is regarded as an arbiter of taste in the world of musical theater.

Did you know? It traces to a Latin noun for an eyewitness, onlooker, or person appointed to settle a dispute, the root of arbitration and arbitrary. Merriam-Webster
