On This Day

November 15, 1777

Congress adopts the Articles of Confederation. The Second Continental Congress approved the Articles of Confederation, the first constitution of the United States, and sent them to the states for ratification two days later. It then took three and a half years to get every state on board (March 1, 1781), and the weak central government they created was later replaced by the U.S. Constitution.

Word of the Day

Merriam-Webster · November 15, 2026

lissome

adjective · \LISS-um\

Audio: Merriam-Webster's official Word of the Day podcast episode (pronunciation and usage).

What it means: Lissome is an adjective used to describe someone or something characterized by easy flexibility and grace.

// He grew up wanting to emulate the lissome skaters he saw competing on television with all their acrobatic leaps and twirls.

In context: “A lissome figure in a sleek black unitard, he is a solitary presence in front of the glowing, fiery red visuals being projected on-screen.” — Mary Brennan, The Herald (Glasgow, Scotland), 30 Aug. 2026

Did you know? Lissome (less commonly lissom) is a gently altered form of its synonym lithesome. Both appeared in the 18th century and trace back to the much older lithe, which first appeared in English in the 14th century and comes from an Old English word meaning “gentle.” Lissome most often describes bodies and their movements, but it also gets used for nimble singing voices, writing styles, guitar playing, and more.
