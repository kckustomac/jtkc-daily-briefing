Word of the Day

Merriam-Webster · November 21, 2026

meet-cute  \MEET-kyoot\  noun

Audio: Merriam-Webster's Word of the Day podcast episode (includes the pronunciation).

Meaning: a cute, charming, or amusing first encounter between romantic partners, whether in a movie or TV show or in real life.

Their meet-cute felt as if it came straight out of a romantic comedy.

“Two women, Dylan and Melanie, cross paths on an artificial beach in a near-apocalyptic meet-cute…” — Natalie Zutter, Literary Hub, 2 Jan. 2026

The word first appeared in the early 1950s; The New York Times Book Review used it in 1952.
