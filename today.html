Word of the Day

Merriam-Webster · November 23, 2026

optimization (noun) · /ahp-tuh-muh-ZAY-shun/

Definition: an act, process, or methodology of making something (such as a design, system, or decision) as good, functional, or effective as possible.

Example: “She traded in her old phone for a newer model that offered improved battery optimization and better camera performance.”

In context: “Somehow, the mess of my notes resists all forms of optimization, or even coherence.” — Rainesford Stauffer, Time, 24 July 2026

Did you know? It comes from optimize (early 19th century) and, like optimum and optimism, from Latin optimus, “best.” Source: Merriam-Webster Word of the Day.
