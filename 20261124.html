Word of the Day

Merriam-Webster · November 24, 2026

dubious · \DOO-bee-us\ · adjective

Meaning: Doubtful or uncertain about something; also, causing doubt, uncertainty, or suspicion. In phrases like “dubious honor” it ironically describes something bad as if it were an achievement.

Despite having some late-season success, we were dubious about the likelihood of our team making the playoffs.

The miraculous revival of their company was achieved through dubious means.

Audio is Merriam-Webster's Word of the Day podcast episode (about 2½ min). Etymology: from Latin dubare, “to hesitate in choice of opinions or courses.” Merriam-Webster
